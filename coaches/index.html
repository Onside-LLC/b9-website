Meet the Coaches

Coaches Who Have Been There

Both lead instructors coach at the college level. When your player takes a lesson at B9, the person in the cage with them is the same person who has spent years deciding which players move on and why.

Hitting Instructor

Zak Ellis

A fifteen-year veteran coach at the Division I JUCO level, Zak has given more than thirty-five years of his life to the game of baseball. He has been responsible for player development at Alvin College for the last fifteen years, where he has helped hundreds of players move on to the next level. Several have gone on to play professionally, as high as the Major Leagues.

Zak was a three-year letterman at Ball High School and spent two years at Eastfield College in Dallas, Texas. His knowledge of kinesiology has made him one of the top hitting instructors in the Houston area.

Zak is also the head coach of the B9 select teams registered through Perfect Game.

Pitching Instructor

Jason Schreiber

A baseball coach at Alvin College since 2000, Jason has sent more than one hundred players to the Division I level and twenty into professional baseball, two of them onto Major League rosters.

He holds a bachelor's degree in sports administration from the University of Houston and a master's degree in fitness and human performance from the University of Houston at Clear Lake.

As a player, Jason was named Houston high school player of the year at Bellaire High School in 1993. His college career included one season at the University of Kansas, one season at San Jacinto Junior College and two seasons at the University of Houston, where he was named to the All-Southwest Conference baseball team in 1996.
