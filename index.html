What We Teach

Programs for Every Player

Whether your player is picking up a bat for the first time or trying out for a select team, we build a plan around their level.

Hitting

One-on-one and small-group swing work focused on mechanics your player can actually feel and repeat.

Pitching

Delivery and command training for new arms and experienced ones, at a pace that protects long-term health.

Fielding & Catching

Fundamentals for every position, from first-step quickness to the details of catching behind the plate.

Select Teams

A path for players ready to compete, with B9 squads registered through Perfect Game.

Meet the Coaches

Coaches Who Have Been There

Both of our lead instructors coach at the college level and have spent years helping players get recruited and drafted.

Hitting Instructor

Zak Ellis

A fifteen-year veteran coach at the Division I JUCO level, Zak has given more than thirty-five years of his life to baseball. Responsible for player development at Alvin College for the last fifteen years, he has helped hundreds of players move on to the next level, including several who reached pro ball as high as the Major Leagues.

Zak was a three-year letterman at Ball High School and spent two years at Eastfield College in Dallas. His knowledge of kinesiology has made him one of the top hitting instructors in the Houston area.

Pitching Instructor

Jason Schreiber

A baseball coach at Alvin College since 2000, Jason has sent more than one hundred players to the Division I level and twenty into the pros, two of them onto Major League rosters.

He holds a bachelor's degree in sports administration from the University of Houston and a master's in fitness and human performance from the University of Houston at Clear Lake.
